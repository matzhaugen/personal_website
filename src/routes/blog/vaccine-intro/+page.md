The vaccination debate is mired in controversy, and without getting too bogged down in the details, which have been covered at length by the references given below, I'll present a few introductory observations for someone new to the field.

Firstly, there is the question of what was responsible for the decline in mortality rates of common childhood diseases in the last 150 years. The answer to that question is that most childhood disease mortality rates had already drastically declined by the time the vaccines were introduced, so vaccines cannot be credited to this observation. A graphical presentation of the historical mortality of these diseases is given by Jordan Henderson's [Gallery of Graphs, 2024](https://jordanhenderson.substack.com/p/gallery-of-graphs). These graphs can also be found in various other material (e.g. by [Humphries and Bystrianyk](https://dissolvingillusions.com/graphs-images/#figures), [Beattie, 2024](https://amzn.asia/d/05IzZhzm), [Kennedy, 2022](https://a.co/d/03Evl3ZN), etc.). Note that this observation says nothing about the efficacy of vaccines, only that something else contributed to the decline in mortality from childhood diseases, e.g. changes in living standards, hygiene, diet, etc. 

Secondly, there is the question of whether vaccination carries a risk of injury from the vaccine itself, irrespective of whether it prevents the disease it purports to prevent or not. To answer this question one would normally consult databases that store these adverse events. One example of these databases is the VAERS in the US, which is made more easily accessible though [OpenVaers.com](https://www.openvaers.com). While these databases show a troubling amount of adverse effects from vaccination, causality between vaccine and reported event cannot be positively ascertained since entries are often self-reported by parents.

More rigorous studies have looked at the potential harm of vaccination, although there are not many of them out there. One such study is by [Mogensen et al, 2017](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/ebiom/article/PIIS2352-39641730046-4/fulltext) who found that "between 3 and 5 months of age, children who received DTP and OPV early had 5-fold higher mortality than still unvaccinated children.". This study has been [discussed](https://vaccinepapers.org/high-mortality-dtp-vaccine/) by others for its clever study design, and a [TED talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_d8PNlXHJ48) by the authors has also been given. Another study looking at neurological disorders found a 6-fold increase in such disorders among the vaccinated group [(Mawson, 2017)](https://functionalforum.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/TONY-MAWSON.pdf). It is notoriously difficult to design a study with no bias between the vaccinated and unvaccinated group as discussed [here](https://vaccinepapers.org/alleged-vax-unvaxed-debunked/). This has lead to a substantial amount of controversy which is summarized by J. Henderson in his essay ["Vaccine Evangelists, Apostates, and Apologists"](https://jordanhenderson.substack.com/p/vaccine-evangelists-apostates-and). For a more in-depth discussion of infant mortality surrounding vaccination, commonly called SIDS (sudden infant death syndrome), see [this blog post](https://www.midwesterndoctor.com/p/a-century-of-evidence-has-accumulated) by A Midwestern Doctor.

Regarding the covid vaccine, a database of peer-reviewed case reports and papers discussing injuries due to covid vaccination is maintained by [React19.org](https://react19.org/science?view=sciencepapers), which now have more than 4500 studies compiled. Biological explanations to why this connection could exist is given by [Palmer et al., 2023](https://doctors4covidethics.org/mrna-vaccine-toxicity/) in their free book called "mRNA Vaccine Toxicity".

Thirdly, there is a question of financial incentives and conflicts of interest. The FDA, which is the agency responsible for regulating the drug industry,  [receives](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-basics/fact-sheet-fda-glance) 45% of the budget from industry through so-called "user-fees". Vaccine products and producers are also largely shielded from liability through the The National Childhood Vaccine Injury Act of 1986 (NCVA). The liability is instead taken by the government, through the courts. The vaccine court has since its inception paid more than 5 Billion dollars out to victims, but only about half of the cases are compensated financially while the other half is dismissed (from the [official statistics](https://www.hrsa.gov/sites/default/files/hrsa/vicp/vicp-stats-06-01-26.pdf)). It is important to note that the Covid vaccines are not included in the covered vaccines by the court, and those injured by those vaccines have to go through the Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program, a much more restrictive administrative program -- from more than 10,000 claims, [~1% are actually awarded compensation](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R46982) (see the documentary "[Follow the Silenced](https://plandemic.com/)" and [this post](https://www.midwesterndoctor.com/p/what-happens-after-a-vaccine-injury) for more info). 

Lastly, I have not touched upon the topic of autism as it relates to vaccines, but that is addressed extensively others (e.g. [Blaxill's Age of Autism](https://a.co/d/0d4ZXyZV) and [Rogers, 2025](https://tobyrogers.substack.com/p/mapping-the-entire-field-of-autism), [2021](https://tobyrogers.substack.com/p/the-political-economy-of-autism)). And one disease that does not have a monotonic decline in the 21st century is polio, which spiked in the 1940s and 50s.  However, there is a striking correlation between pesticide production (DDT) and polio occurrence in the US, although causation cannot be established based only on this data ([see Jim West's research](https://harvoa.org/polio/index.htm#indyRes)). Polio is extensively discussed in [The Moth in the Iron Lung](https://a.co/d/0iXV2h2p) by Forrest Maready.
